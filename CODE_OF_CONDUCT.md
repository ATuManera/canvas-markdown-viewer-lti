# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a straightforward, respectful experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that makes this project better:

- being clear and direct without being unkind;
- accepting that a technical disagreement is not a personal one;
- saying "I was wrong" when you were;
- helping someone who is new to LTI, to Canvas, or to this codebase;
- writing down the reasoning, not just the conclusion.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- insults, personal attacks, or dismissing someone rather than their argument;
- harassment, public or private;
- publishing someone's private information without permission;
- anything a reasonable professional would consider inappropriate at work.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages —
and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainers at **conducta@atumanera.pe**. Every report is handled
privately, and the person who reported it is not identified without their consent.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code and issues that do not follow this document,
and will say why when they do. Repeated or serious breaches lead to a temporary or permanent
ban from participation.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this document in good faith may face consequences
decided by the project's leadership.
